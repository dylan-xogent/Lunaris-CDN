# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems. Email **dylan.johnston@xogent.com** with a description, reproduction steps, and the affected URL or component. You'll get an acknowledgement within a few business days.

## Reporting abuse

Lunaris CDN prohibits malware, illegal content, and infrastructure abuse (see the [Terms of Service](https://lunaris.win/tos)). To report a file or project hosted on the service, email **dylan.johnston@xogent.com** with the full CDN URL. Reported content is reviewed and removed, and repeat offenders are terminated.
